FollowFrame / guide

How to keep an Instagram feed always on top in Windows.

A narrow, movable frame can keep chosen visual updates nearby without covering the work underneath.

Set up the floating frame

  1. Download and open FollowFrame for Windows x64.
  2. Connect the experimental Personal Instagram source inside the app, or choose a local media folder.
  3. Keep Always on top enabled, move the 9:16 window beside your primary workspace, and choose a comfortable playback pace.

Keep control

Pause playback, mute videos, refresh on demand, or disable always-on-top from Settings. FollowFrame remains a separate utility rather than changing your normal Instagram browser session.
