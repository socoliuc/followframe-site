FollowFrame / support

A direct line when the frame needs attention.

FollowFrame is small and independent. Support messages go to a real project mailbox, not a data-collection form.

Get help

Email support@followframe.com with your FollowFrame version, Windows version, what you expected, and what happened. Do not send Instagram passwords, cookies, private media, or session files.

Security reports

Send security issues privately to security@followframe.com. The current disclosure address is also published in security.txt.

Keep the free app moving

Voluntary support is welcome through PayPal. It does not unlock features or change support priority.
