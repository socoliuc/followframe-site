FollowFrame / independent utility

A quiet media surface beside the work that matters.

FollowFrame was built for people who want the accounts and media they chose nearby without surrendering the desktop to another feed.

What it is

A compact, always-on-top 9:16 Windows player with local playback controls, manual or scheduled refresh, and one active source at a time.

What it is not

FollowFrame is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta. It is not an analytics dashboard, follower-growth service, account automation tool, or cloud media archive.

Where it is going

The source-agnostic core begins with local folders. The product remains free, with voluntary support and at most one clearly disclosed website sponsor.

Launch assets

A sanitized 48-second launch cut shows the source connection, refresh, playback, settings, and local-session boundary without private account content.
